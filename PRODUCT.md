# HEADCOUNT — operations console

**What it is.** An idle game whose economy is the player's attention, played
through a factory operations console. Workers complete tasks but raise
questions; the player answers at a fixed rate. An AI agent (on TrueForge)
designs the game's mechanics; every change is simulated, approved, and lands in
this console mid-shift.

**Who uses it.** A player-operator at a desk, and hackathon judges evaluating
whether the harness work is real. Both are in a task: run the floor, or verify
the claim.

**Register.** Product. The console must read as a professional-grade
operations tool — SCADA panel, not mobile game. Trust comes from density,
precision, and restraint. Delight lives in the simulation, not the chrome.

**Scene.** Dark control room, one operator, evening shift, second monitor.
Dark theme is functional: red saturation states must read instantly.

**Non-goals.** Marketing flourish, onboarding tours, sound.
